# Draft invitation to try Code198x

Would you like to try a small programming activity with Code198x?

You can change a greeting in BASIC or investigate how an assembly program changes
a Spectrum's screen border. Both start in the browser, with explanations for
people who have not programmed before and optional questions if you already have.

Start here: https://code198x.com/try/

You can explore on your own, with your children or with a group of students.
There are notes for parents and teachers, and a session sheet if you want to
keep predictions and observations on paper. Choose one activity; there is no
time target or requirement to finish a course.

I would like to know what makes sense and where the material leaves you guessing.
If you try it, tell me which activity you chose, where you needed help and what
you wanted to do next. You do not need to write a formal review. Please leave
learner names and other identifying details out of anything shared publicly.
