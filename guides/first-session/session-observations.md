# Record a first session observation

Use this when someone tries the public pack. No session has been recorded yet.
Keep the original prediction and the observed result distinct. The purpose is
to find what helps someone understand and continue, not to award a score.

Record the date, activity and lesson address, relevant prior programming
experience, device/browser and whether someone helped. Describe the setting
without learner names or identifying details.

For each meaningful step, record:

- What the learner expected before running or reading the answer.
- What they did and what the machine displayed, or what they traced on paper.
- Their explanation in their own words, including uncertainty.
- Any help requested or supplied, and which hint or instruction helped.
- Whether they made the change, kept their work and checked the saved copy.

Distinguish an independent explanation, an explanation after reading the answer,
work completed with coaching, incomplete work and unclear evidence. A copied
working listing alone does not establish understanding. Do not generalise a
single session to every learner or device.

Record what they wanted to try next. If the pack was hard to navigate, retain
the link text and destination they expected. For a technical failure, retain the
page, action, actual message and configuration. State the symptom without
inventing a mechanism.

File reproducible problems in the repository that owns them and link the original
umbrella issue. Check for an existing issue first. Separate tool failures from
confusing explanations and from a learner's unfinished exploration. After a
repair, repeat the affected step rather than demanding the whole session again.

Pause pack expansion while collecting these first observations. Existing
activities can be repaired as evidence warrants. The parent issues remain open;
publication and automated checks do not replace a learner trial.
