# Yearfall — source and economy review

Inspected the existing `yearfall/unit-06/yearfall.bas`, its retained brief and
current catalogue. These are source findings, separate from new execution.

- The original starts with 100 people and permits at most six newcomers per
  year for ten years. Its 200-person Wise Ruler result is unreachable; the
  theoretical no-death maximum is 160.
- The source permits ten planted acres per opening person. The old brief says
  one acre per person and describes different yields and population growth.
- Planting uses the opening population, while starvation is calculated later.
  A plan can therefore use labour from people the same turn then removes.
- Food, land and seed are entered separately, with no combined editable review.
  Inputs permit fractional allocations, despite whole-person and acre reports.
- Feeding above need consumes grain without affecting the random newcomer
  count. The report should make this lack of benefit clear.

The approved trial retains the resource-management idea and finite run. It
reconciles units and resolution order, makes the draft budget inspectable and
replaces score thresholds with reports of actual outcomes. The old lesson
endpoint remains intact pending native acceptance and new lesson authoring.
