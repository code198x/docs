## What develops here

Start a new program. Continuous updates, signed movement, arrays, custom characters and checking before changing state have precedents in Volley, Touchdown, Sonar and Crates. Recall their contracts locally; do not require the reader to remember an earlier listing or import an unexplained engine. This is not the route's first custom-graphics project.

The new relationship is **a body made from remembered head positions**. Its order answers which square leaves next. Occupancy answers whether a square is already used. Growth means keeping the old tail while adding a new head. These ideas need separate runnable examples before they share the finished loop.

Teach two small representation changes explicitly. First move a short list's coordinates; then keep the coordinates in place and move the indices around reusable slots. Later replace a search through body positions with an occupancy grid. Each change must preserve visible rules. Count the work before claiming a speed improvement, and measure the actual BASIC checkpoints before making timing claims.

## Making the circular array concrete

Begin with four live positions in slots 1–4: tail index 1, head index 4. A non-growing move discards slot 1 from the live body, advances the tail to 2, and writes the new head into slot 5. The coordinates in slots 2–4 do not move. The old value in slot 1 may remain in memory; it is outside the live body.

Later, with four live cells in slots 9–12, a normal move advances the tail to 10 and wraps the head to 1. The live order becomes 10, 11, 12, 1. If that move grows instead, the tail stays at 9 and the live order becomes 9, 10, 11, 12, 1. Array indices describe storage; the coordinate values describe the arena. Wrapping storage does not wrap the snake through a wall.

Use a labelled slot diagram and a trace of an actual checkpoint for the lesson. This prose example explains the representation; it is not an emulator observation. Keep length explicit: when all twelve slots are live, the next head slot is the current tail slot, and whether that slot may be reused depends on whether the tail leaves. The accepted game ends as soon as length reaches twelve.
