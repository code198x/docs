# Shadowkeep — the inhabitants revisit: "The Waking" (vision, 2026-06-12)

Steve's vision, bottled as Stage-1 input for whenever this module earns its slot. Working
title *The Waking* (provisional; module was scoped as "inhabitants" in the brief's
trajectory table).

## The vision

The keep is **full of inhabitants, frozen in stasis**. The goal: figure out how to unfreeze
each of them in turn. Part Magicland Dizzy, part Sleeping Beauty. (Stolen, knowingly, from
an idea previously pushed toward the text adventure.)

## Why it works (the read)

- **Frozen inhabitants are NPCs without AI.** The scoped "cell-based NPCs + threat" problem
  — fair threat-AI in a deterministic cell keep — is deleted, not solved. A person in stasis
  is a state object: the engine's native species. *A person is a lamp you light differently*
  — the track's through-line (Gloaming's lamplighter → the thief who wakes a keep), the same
  verb shape scaled from infrastructure to people.
- **The retcon is lore.** The Place's lit, furnished, silent rooms were never empty — everyone
  was asleep the whole time. The shipped first chapter retroactively gains its mystery.
  "The Keep Stands" → "The Keep Wakes."
- **It merges two revisits.** "Inhabitants" + "items that matter" collapse into one design:
  each unfreezing is an object-puzzle chain (what wakes the cook; the woken cook unlocks the
  blacksmith) — Dizzy-shaped fetch/use dependency graphs; puzzle design as data structures.
- **Ancestors:** Magicland Dizzy (Oliver Twins, Spectrum, frozen characters as rescue
  objects); Sleeping Beauty (the whole-keep stasis, public domain); the Atic Atac collection-
  quest tradition already in the bar table.

## Tensions and guardrails

- **The parser anti-goal stands** (no parser, no dialogue trees — The Lantern Path's rung).
  Object puzzles only; at most a one-line ROM-font greeting per woken soul.
- **The Lantern Path loses its stasis seed** — that game needs a new concept eventually.
  Noted, not solved.
- **Cap the cast** (the toolbar-cap lesson): a handful of inhabitants with a designed
  dependency graph, not a census.
- **Open:** does the curse's source exist in-game? One environmental hazard (the briar — the
  thing that froze them, still active) would add a loss-edge with zero AI; or it stays a
  peaceful puzzle-explorer like the Place. Decide at its Stage 1.
