# Exodus — design vision (2026-06-12)

Steve's vision, captured verbatim in substance; input to the Stage-1 brief revision
alongside [design-stress-2026-06-12.md](design-stress-2026-06-12.md). Builds on Flock.

## The vision

**We're still getting the sheep home.** The sheep are canon, not placeholder — Flock was one
sheep crossing one road; Exodus is the flock's great journey home. Side-on view, cartoon
sheep, a Copper sky.

**The mountain.** Levels are stacked hazard bands worked downwards (a descent from the
summit) or upwards (a climb) — the flock takes a **side-to-side switchback path** up or down
the screen to the destination. Maximum journey on a single screen; the vertical axis is the
visible progress meter. Roads and rivers still appear as bands.

**A little parallax.** Depth and a living sky — resolved below as *blitted cloud drift*, not
dual playfield.

**The verb model.** The player marks up the route with **tool icons** that tell the sheep
what to do at each stage; on release, the sheep execute the plan. Direct terrain
modification TBC.

## The synthesis (agreed direction for the revision)

1. **Markers fire Blitter terrain ops through the sheep** — the Lemmings unification. The
   player doesn't click-carve the cliff; they mark the spot and the *sheep digs the cutting*
   when it arrives (a cookie-cut blit), marks the gap and the sheep *builds the causeway*
   (Blitter copies, plank by plank). Every consequential marker is a Blitter op landing
   during the reveal. **The Blitter stays the puzzle's verb** — the rung's thesis holds —
   while the player's plan becomes an icon list. Behaviour markers (turn, wait/go) share the
   toolbar.
2. **The icon model resolves design-stress B2 and B3 outright** (icons are removable = undo;
   icons persist = retry keeps the plan) and shrinks B1's surface (less raw terrain surgery).
   B1 still needs the abandon control; D1 (the emotion choice) is untouched and still the
   Stage-1 decision.
3. **Parallax = drifting cloud bobs at two or three speeds** — ambient blitted motion using
   this rung's own technique. Dual-playfield parallax stays reserved for the Parallax module
   (rung boundary respected). Copper sky as already designed.
4. **Toolbar cap: four to five icons** — proposed for the anti-goals. Lemmings managed on
   eight; the toolbar is the new scope-creep axis and the cap goes in writing before the
   prototype tempts us.
5. **Descent and ascent levels** are the free variety axis: descending levels make fall
   height the standing threat; ascending levels are build-led.

## What the revision must reconcile

- Brief §3 (verbs are direct carve/raise clicks) → verbs are *marker-delivered* carve/raise
  plus behaviour markers. §4, §8, §9 re-themed to the mountain.
- The budget (rationed edits) maps to rationed *icons* — per-type counts, Lemmings-style.
- Design-stress gates re-checked against the new model (most improve; B1 abandon and D1
  emotion remain).
- A winnability script becomes an icon list + release — note for the gate's choreography.

## Decisions (2026-06-12, same day — confirmed with Steve)

1. **Emotion: threshold + stars (the Lemmings answer).** A level *passes* at a threshold
   (indicatively 3 of 5 home); bringing **all five** home earns the level's star; an
   all-stars journey earns the true "every sheep home" ending. Loss-aversion operates on
   every release (a death costs the star you keep), retry stays free and light, imperfect
   runs no longer force re-watches, and the perfection chase **is** the go-again hook.
   Resolves design-stress D1 and the missing Finish hook in one move.

2. **Toolbar: five icons, capped.** Arc 1: **CUT** (Blitter carve — the sheep digs a
   downward ramp through the band, in its walking direction), **FENCE** (mini-raise — a
   blitted post; the flock turns: the switchback maker and the anti-trap tool), **BROLLY**
   (behaviour — the next fall is survived; its existence *is* the fatal-fall rule's
   telegraph, resolving P1). Arc 2: **BRIDGE** (Blitter raise — a causeway built
   plank-by-plank until it meets land), **WAIT** (behaviour — a fixed, visible hold count;
   the road-timing tool). The five-icon cap goes into the anti-goals.

3. **Direct terrain modification: dropped.** All terrain change arrives through markers,
   fired by the sheep on arrival. One input model, clean undo (remove the icon), no
   misclick-spends-budget surface. The vision's TBC is closed.

**Still open for Stage 1 (small):** the abandon control during release (stress B1 — the
one BREAKS the icon model doesn't dissolve); the bridge-pricing law written into the
level-design section (D4); rationing shape (per-icon counts per level, Lemmings-style).
