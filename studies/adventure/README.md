# Adventure / Colossal Cave — second game study

**Initial draft for owner review, 18 October 2026. Not integrated or deployed.** The owner chose Adventure after Spacewar, with interest in the Spectrum adventure scene and later graphical storytelling. Connections should develop from the material rather than become a required itinerary.

Read [the study](study.md), then [evidence and limits](evidence.md). [Connections](connections.md) records useful existing destinations; [the named-subject audit](vault-audit.md) reuses the candidate backlog. [Observations](observations.json) records a bounded executable encounter, with [a repeatable script](observe.py). [The plan](../../plans/2026-10-18-adventure-study.md) bounds this work.

This pass explains play through location descriptions, recognised commands, remembered object state, a locked route, mapping, resource pressure, puzzles, scoring and expedition structure. It follows real caving, collaborative survey work, Crowther’s original and Woods’s expansion, source exchange and a documented BBC home adaptation. The chosen executable is explicitly a later C port of the 350-point game. Source inspection and bounded execution do not amount to a complete playthrough or human review.

The single original diagram is an explanation, not a screenshot or complete map. It reveals the early grate obstacle only. Colourful editorial imagery, selected terminal captures, deeper puzzle/recovery observations and human reading/play remain further work before considering publication. The draft does not claim an absolute first text adventure/RPG, turn proposed courses into current delivery, or assert a necessary progression from words to pictures.

Adventure game text is credited to Will Crowther and Don Woods; the observed modern port is by Arthur O’Dwyer. Short extracts illustrate criticism and review. All rights in the underlying game remain with their respective owners. No game source, binary, full transcript, audio, cave map or borrowed screenshot is distributed.
