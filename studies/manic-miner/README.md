# Manic Miner — whole-game study draft

Read [the study](study.md), [evidence](evidence.md) and [Vault audit](vault-audit.md). Follows original play, movement, room design, music, influences, production, publishing, the sequels and community creation. Prepared alongside Lemmings using the approved article composition and new original editorial artwork.

Owner review and publication approval remain open. No new course or remake is commissioned. Selected execution is not a complete playthrough. Rights in Manic Miner (Matthew Smith/Bug-Byte, 1983, ZX Spectrum) remain with their owners; the capture illustrates criticism/review. No game media, firmware, audio or full map is distributed.
