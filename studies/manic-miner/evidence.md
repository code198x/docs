# Manic Miner: evidence and limits

Research and illustration pass: 18 October 2026. Reader-facing scope is Matthew Smith's 1983 Spectrum original, its demonstrated influences and the change into Jet Set Willy, later versions and community authoring. Draft for owner review, not historical sign-off or a complete playthrough.

## Executed

## Claims and interpretation

- Opening arrangement: inspected own capture. Rules and wider cavern behaviour: original-program disassembly and existing reference article's original source trail, not asserted execution through all caverns.
- Jump: inspected annotated original routines 35515/35805. The rising/falling sequence is explicit; input/direction handling is separate from the airborne trajectory. No exact pixel/frame measurements or claim about every collision edge case in this draft. Illustrative figure is not an engine reconstruction.
- Eugene and Kong: inspected routines 36344/37403. Eugene checks whether items remain; Kong switch routine checks Willy's position. Cavern table confirms names and sequence. Wider beam detail follows the original-program/reference account, not a new complete playthrough.
- Music: existing Vault retains contemporary reporting and tune identifications; inspected disassembly's sound index and movement routines show program-driven sound. No new listening experiment or first-in-game-music superlative claimed.
- Origins: Smith's 2005 answer names Donkey Kong and Hogue's Atari work. Transcript misspells Hogue's name and includes interviewer claims which are not adopted. Cross-check Hogue identity with his own interview. The Atari title is identified as Miner 2049er; do not quote the erroneous transcript name as a second person.
- Red notebook, shop work and sequel pressure: Smith's retrospective testimony, attributed, not a medical inference or independent biography.
- Product/release/development/publishing: retain the detailed original source trail in games/manic-miner. PCN review dated 11–17 August 1983 and other summer notices support summer availability; do not assert a new exact release day. The studio/copyright history is not reconstructed from one recollection.
- JSW: CRASH 4 review and CRASH 9 correspondence establish connected-room play and contemporary faults/corrections. No need to reproduce every bug or make all of them one Attic failure.
- JSW II: inspected Rowson/Wetherill participant comments via Wiseman, including CPC expansion and porting. Community material and disassemblies establish continued activity, not permission to republish assets.
- Route/time examples and design explanations are ours; not observed player-study results, universal prescriptions or historical creator motives.
