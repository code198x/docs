# Boulder Dash — evidence, interpretation and limits

18 October 2026. Canonical illustrated draft for owner review. Historical sign-off and publication remain open. Original Atari release is the centre; ports, sequels, editors and particular regional adaptations are distinguished. No complete cave or game completion is claimed.

## Original documents inspected

- Tom Hudson, ANALOG Computing 20, July 1984, printed p.19 (PDF p.21). Text extracted and page rendered/visually inspected. Confirms 32K tape/disk at $29.95, 16K cartridge at $39.95, sixteen caves, five settings, intermissions, changed arrangements/targets, creatures, idle animation, tile-step criticism and Exidy arcade adaptation. The review is a contemporary witness, not proof of publisher superlatives.
- CRASH 12, January 1985, pp.74–75, preserved full review read. Front Runner/K-Tel, £7.95, scrolling, A–P structure, commentary and ratings are Spectrum-specific. 98% is playability (also addictive qualities), not its 93% overall score. Its resemblance/originality rhetoric is not adopted as an influence finding.
- Construction Kit manual: empty text layer; rendered printed pp.4–11, especially pp.8–11. Confirms separate construction mode, palette, creating/editing caves/intermissions, saving and sequence editing. Later editor/manual does not establish that every original release shares every added element.
- Liepa interview transcription, published November 2022, read in full: prototype, Gray/The Pit disagreement, Choplifter silhouette inspiration, enlargement/scrolling, book-like advance/royalty arrangement and later work. It is retrospective testimony. Do not repeat a Forth claim or an uncertain memory of a 4/8K figure as established implementation history.
- Rights-holder history, Deluxe and current anniversary page read, as dated promotional/participant sources. Avoid their universal first-home-game-to-arcade claim, inconsistent release-platform chronology, self-congratulation and unsupported total-sales claims.
- Švelch's 2021 original research article read. Spurný, Gavar/VBG and Bórik cases are specific to Czechoslovakia and particular machines. Do not generalise law, access, informal circulation or taste across the entire Eastern Bloc. The article's hardware/scrolling explanation is not independently benchmarked here.
- Artsoft's own about/manual pages inspected for engine traditions and editor scope. No community level bundles or third-party art copied. Historical map resemblance alone does not establish a creator-named influence.

`source-identities.json` records copy hashes; the study's bibliography supplies public sources. Original scans stay outside the public repositories.

## Execution

The prepared older Fleet WASM artefact (build metadata revision 1b999de47dea60e424efbdc68f1cd483cb0191a6, modified=true) booted titles but did not yield reliable input-driven gameplay in this attempt. It is not the final capture tool. VICE was probed headlessly as a possible independent fallback; none of its images is included.

Built the unchanged current Emu198x native headless runner at revision 6cc5d5f9e7fe6507a079f237bc44da0dc95fe25c, release profile with no UI default features, locked/offline. No emulator source edits or new dependencies. Held 16 KiB cartridge, original Atari XL OS revision 2, NTSC 800XL model, BASIC disabled. Exact hashes in captures/manifest.json. A failed XEX attempt is not used or described as a game defect.

Start/Fire enters the first cave. The final script waits for reveal, then makes Down, Left and Right movements. Own captures show the opening arrangement and a cleared vertical passage with Rockford and changed settled rocks. This verifies selected early digging/changed layout, not every creature rule, exact update order, timing on real hardware or all later caves. `observations.json` preserves the bounded input schedule without supplying private media or states.

The diagram of removing support is a hand-authored explanation, not an executed physics trace: the digger is beside the dirt square, not diagonally adjacent or beneath the rock. It omits rolling neighbours. The paired invented caves do not reproduce commercial layouts or promise a unique solution. No source code from a clone or original executable is reused.
