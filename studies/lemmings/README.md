# Lemmings — whole-game study draft

Read [the study](study.md), [evidence](evidence.md) and [Vault audit](vault-audit.md). Follows play, skills, time, terrain, art, collaborative production, influences, ports, sequels, mobile redesign and community tools. Prepared alongside Manic Miner using the approved article composition and original explanatory diagrams/artwork.

Owner review and publication approval remain open. No new course or remake is commissioned. Selected execution is not a complete playthrough. Rights in Lemmings (DMA Design/Psygnosis, 1991, Amiga) remain with their owners. No game disks, firmware, music, third-party screenshots or complete levels are distributed.
