# Lemmings: evidence and limits

Research and illustration pass: 18 October 2026. Reader-facing scope is the original Amiga game, the experience of indirect control, art and collaborative production, demonstrated influences and the different paths taken by later products and community tools. Draft for owner review, not historical sign-off or a complete playthrough.

## Original documents and scope

Inspected Psygnosis's 36-page combined Lemmings/Oh No! More Lemmings manual (copyright 1991), English printed pp.11–17 and illustrated introductory pages 4–10. The scanned text layer is empty; rendered English pages were OCRed and inspected. Record this as a later combined manual, not the first-release inlay. Its task descriptions, twelve-brick builder, skill limits, panel, pause, minimap, passwords, play categories, platform controls and Amiga two-player rules are directly readable.

- Manual distinguishes the Amiga's second mouse from the ST's alternate second-player controls, and says PC has no two-player mode. Do not let a broad mouse anecdote override these version differences.
- Bomber is described as one way to remove blockers. The draft does not reproduce the existing reference entry's absolute “only” claim. This study does not require a complete taxonomy of blocker/terrain edge cases.

- 50 × 80% = 40 is deliberately invented arithmetic, not an assertion about Just Dig's actual rescue target.

## History and interpretation

Dailly's revision-17 participant history, Hammond's studio account and existing contemporary source trail distinguish collaborative creation from a single-origin anecdote. The accounts disagree on some exact moments/credits; retain that uncertainty. The study does not assume the team watched Disney's White Wilderness. Alaska Fish and Game supports the short correction about real animals; the false mass-suicide story is not taught as biology.

The existing reference article retains original magazine evidence (*The One* 28, *Amiga Format* 20, *ZZAP!* 72), Tim Wright's *Arcade* 2 testimony, port counts, early prices and later-series details. These original documents have not all been newly reread in full for this pass. The draft identifies the reused source trail explicitly; it does not misrepresent a modern lookup as another independent original witness. Avoid precise sales totals, unverified lawsuit claims and the assertion that all replacement music was public domain.

The named multiplayer influence of Populous/Stunt Car Racer is Dailly's retrospective account, specific to multiplayer experiments. The painting/editor relationship is similarly participant testimony. Readability, worker attention, partial-success targets and interfaces are our interpretations of concrete rules, not creator quotations, universal design laws or measured player-study results.

Inspected ST NEWS's 1993 contemporary Lemmings 2 account: it describes selecting among twelve tribes, practice, different skill sets and the ST version. Its careless national label for DMA is not repeated; Hammond and the credited studio history establish Dundee. Sony's 20 March 2006 PSP announcement supports Team17, editing and sharing; its May date is an announced North American date, not a worldwide first release. Sad Puppy's 20 December 2018 publisher-hosted launch account supports portrait/touch design, camera decisions and in-app purchases; no current price, store availability or continued support is promised. NeoLemmix's own project account distinguishes Lemmix reproduction from its own later goals and features.
