## Current publishing boundary

The umbrella imagery decision (`decisions/publishing-third-party-imagery.md` in the 198x umbrella) says **“Portraits are published only where the subject supplies or agrees to one”**, allows magazine page images by exception where layout is the subject, and prohibits cropping published artwork to taste. Its source-finding restriction also needs reconciling with externally sourced portraits before publication. The earlier hardware-photo approval is specific to hardware. This record does not amend those rules.

For portraits, a copyright licence and the project's subject-agreement requirement are separate checks. For advertising, first inspect the actual page and identify the work/credit, then settle permission or an explicit project exception for the particular excerpt, purpose and size. Cropping out neighbouring editorial material is different from silently altering the advertiser's artwork, but neither has been approved here. Do not apply the cover-art allowance to magazine advertisements automatically.

Any eventual image should carry its own credit and rights record: source page and original publication, creator, date, licence/permission or stated exception, crop coordinates, resulting dimensions and alterations. A ShareAlike photograph remains separately licensed; it must not be swept into the repository's general prose licence.
