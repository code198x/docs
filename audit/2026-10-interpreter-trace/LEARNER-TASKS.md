# Try an interpreter trace

Read From Source to Silicon unit 2, “Translating As You Go”. You can arrive there
without reading earlier units. Use paper or a text editor; no installation is
needed. Keep answer panels closed until you have recorded your prediction.

1. In the shown loop, start `total` at 5 and change the last counter value to 4.
   Keep the addition at one. Write the total after each visit, the final output
   and the number of times the addition statement runs.
2. Keep those four visits, but change the addition from one to two. Predict the
   new trace and output. Explain what changed and what stayed the same.
3. Someone resets `total` to 5 at the start of every visit. Predict the output
   with additions of two. Explain the first point where this differs from task 2.
4. A helper says, “Four additions means four processor instructions, and we know
   how much time the interpreter added.” What does the paper trace show?
   What extra evidence would be needed for each claim?
5. Optional: read [Doing It Again](https://code198x.com/foundations/repeating/unit-01/).
   If the range becomes 1 to 0, does the initial total still reach the final SHOW?
   Explain using the stated loop rule.

Record first answers and revisions separately. Say what made you change your
mind. A parent or teacher can note requests for help without supplying the answer.
The [assessor notes](ASSESSOR.md) keep expected reasoning separate from your work.
