# Code198x website content audit — September 2026

**Audited:** website working tree at `341c5f0c` plus uncommitted changes (54 modified, 24 untracked files), 23 September 2026. The live site at code198x.com was checked where it differs. **Output:** findings only; no site files were changed. Checklist: [TASKS.md](TASKS.md). Evidence: [data/](data/).

## Summary

The site's machinery is sound. Tests, the production build and all eleven content checks pass. The crawl of 2,473 built pages and 998 redirects found three broken internal links, no broken redirects, and no images missing alt text. Every module catalogue matches its lesson files.

The problems are editorial. Six stand out:

1. **The Vault is wrong about one claim in six.** Nobody has reviewed any of its 1,478 entries. A 30-entry sample found 40 of 250 checked claims contradicted by sources (16%), and 22 of the 30 entries contain at least one error, including the ZX Spectrum, ZX81, ULA, Clive Sinclair and Steven Vickers entries a CRASH! Live visitor is likely to open. Each page does carry an honest “Not yet fact-checked” footer.
2. **Planned curriculum is presented as available.** 66 placeholder game pages are live and in the sitemap, with fixed unit counts and prerequisite chains through games that do not exist. The C64, Amiga, NES and AMOS track pages tell readers to “start with Game 1 and work through in order” over lists that are mostly “Coming soon”.
3. **207 Vault pages refer readers to a source collection they cannot see.** They use phrases such as “held here”, “this library” and “this corpus”. This breaks the rule that public content must not mention private collections.
4. **Readers never see 94% of Vault citations.** The template renders citations only for date fields, and 2,055 of the 2,185 frontmatter citations attach to other claims. A further 1,014 entries (69%) cite nothing.
5. **Three Spectrum lessons contain factual errors.** The Spectrum curriculum is otherwise accurate: 58 of 70 checked rows verified, with no wrong claims in Meet Assembly.
6. **About 630 lesson pages share the wrong search description:** “Learn assembly by building games…”, including every BASIC, Foundations and Craft lesson.

## Findings

Severity: **High** misleads a reader or breaks a project rule on a public surface. **Medium** degrades quality or trust at scale. **Low** is housekeeping.

### What checked out

- Tests (`vitest`), the production build and all eleven content checks pass: route, template whitespace, Vault imagery, links, sources, dates, prose dates, platform balance, duplicates, NES assembler and frontmatter quoting.
- No broken redirects, no images without alt text and no missing images.
- Every unit catalogue matches its lesson files. No retired Spectrum BASIC lesson survives as a page, and no lesson has a future `pubDate`.
- The Vault’s “Not yet fact-checked” footer is accurate on every entry.
- Meet Assembly, Meet BASIC and Meteor Storm are largely accurate: the only confirmed errors are H7 (a) to (c).
- Living/dead status was correct in every sampled Vault entry.

## What was not done

- **The full-route accessibility sweep** (about 25 minutes, and it writes to `dist/`) was not run; only the 27 gated template pages were checked. The last full sweep, on 20 August, found no serious violations across 2,239 routes.
- **Fact-checking was sampled, not exhaustive.** Of the Spectrum curriculum, four modules were checked in full and six sampled. Of the Vault, 30 of 1,478 entries were checked. Some Vault verdicts rest on web sources, often Wikipedia; the sample file flags them. C64, Amiga, NES, Foundations and Craft lessons, patterns, From the Metal and Field Notes were not fact-checked.
- **External links** (the site links to about 30 outside domains) were not checked for rot.
- **Live and local differ in two ways:** local `main` is three commits ahead of origin, with four Game Feel lessons awaiting review that return 404 live; and the new browser player is untracked. Findings describe the working tree unless marked live.
- **The scratch build skipped Pagefind indexing,** so search was not audited, and the crawl’s one `/pagefind/…` “broken link” is an artefact of the build, not a real fault.
