# Fact-check: ZX Spectrum curriculum (CRASH! Live launch path)

Read-only spot-check of hardware and historical claims in
`website/src/content/curriculum/sinclair-zx-spectrum/`, checked against the
publications listed under "Sources used". Audit date: 2026-09-23.

## Summary

| Verdict | Count |
|---|---|
| WRONG | 3 |
| IMPRECISE | 4 |
| UNSUPPORTED | 5 |
| VERIFIED | 58 rows |
| **Total** | **70 rows** |

Counts are table rows. Some VERIFIED rows group several related claims, such as
V29 (worked arithmetic), V47 (key legends) and V58 (memory and launch facts), so
more than 58 claims were verified.

**Scope covered:** all 8 units of `assembly/meet-assembly`; all 24 units of
`assembly/meteor-storm`; all 15 units of `basic/meet-basic`; `getting-started.mdx`;
a sample from `assembly/meet-the-machine` (the 16-unit series marked `complete`) and
`machine/meet-the-machine` (5 units, marked `in-progress`); and a sample from other
BASIC games (night-patrol, tail-chase, brick-bash). The brief named
"assembly/meet-the-machine (Meet Assembly — 8 lessons)". Two different series exist:
`meet-assembly` has the 8 lessons, and `meet-the-machine` exists both under
`assembly/` and under `machine/`. All three were checked, the Meet the Machine series
by sample.

**Sources used:**

- **Z80:** Zilog, *Z80 CPU User Manual*, document UM008011-0816 (a late printing; cited below as "Zilog UM0080"), <https://www.zilog.com/docs/z80/um0080.pdf>. Page numbers are from its contents list.
- **BASIC manual:** Steven Vickers, ed. Robin Bradbeer, *ZX Spectrum BASIC Programming*, 2nd edn, Sinclair Research, 1983. Page numbers are printed page numbers. In the copy consulted some tables are illegible, and chapter 25 (the system-variable table) is missing.
- **ROM:** Ian Logan and Frank O'Hara, *The Complete Spectrum ROM Disassembly* (Melbourne House, 1983), cited by ROM address and routine label; and *The Guide to the Spectrum ROM* (Hilderbay), cited by chapter and page.
- **ULA and video timing:** Chris Smith, *The ZX Spectrum ULA: How to Design a Microcomputer* (ZX Design and Media, 2010), cited by chapter, table, figure and printed page: ch.6 (pp.71–74), ch.10 (pp.87–94), ch.11 (tables 11-1 and 11-2, pp.96–100), ch.15 (pp.137–146), ch.18 (pp.189–207) and ch.21 (p.227).
- **History:** Colin Woodcock, *The ZX Spectrum on Your PC*, 2nd edn (2012), appendix A, "Key Sinclair/Spectrum dates from the last 30 years", p.133; and Alessandro Grussu, *Spectrumpedia*, vol.1, English edn (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).

**Other findings (no verdict):**

- **No `<Sources>` sections in either Meet the Machine series.** All 16 units of `assembly/meet-the-machine` and all 5 units of `machine/meet-the-machine` lack one. The unit specification (`docs/specifications/unit.md`, "Source notes") expects a section on every page. `machine/meet-the-machine/unit-01.mdx` carries dated, priced historical claims with no citation. `unit-02.mdx` carries timing figures, one of them wrong (W1), with no citation.
- **Near-verbatim wording.** `machine/meet-the-machine/unit-01.mdx:26` closely follows the timeline sentence in Woodcock, *The ZX Spectrum on Your PC*, 2nd edn, appendix A, p.133 ("Clive Sinclair announces the launch of the ZX Spectrum at the Earls Court Computer Show… orders will be delivered in 'two weeks'"). It needs paraphrase or attribution as well as a citation.
- **Private paths:** none found. A search of every Spectrum `.mdx` for local library paths and private collection names found none; the only path-like hit was a public site link, `/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/basic-reference/`.
- **Superlatives:** no "first", "only" or "invented" historical claims found on the checked path. One comparative claim appears at `assembly/meet-the-machine/unit-15.mdx:14`: "here the Spectrum stands apart from every other machine in this course". Within the course's four systems (C64, NES, Amiga, Spectrum) the claim holds, but I checked only the Spectrum side against a source.
- **Emulator version conflict:** `basic/meet-basic/index.mdx:29` requires "Emu198x v0.22.1 or later", but `basic/meet-basic/unit-01-make-the-spectrum-answer.mdx:22` says "v0.22.0 or later".
- **Toolchain conflict:** `getting-started.mdx:19–39` presents "pasmo syntax" builds as `asm198x --dialect pasmonext --sna` and `pasmonext --sna`, and calls PasmoNext "the reference the Asm198x dialect is checked against". Every Meet Assembly and Meteor Storm lesson uses `--dialect pasmo --cpu z80 --tapbas` and upstream Pasmo. This is a consistency problem for launch readers, not a hardware fact.
- **Unverifiable tooling claims:** tool-install claims in `getting-started.mdx` are not covered by the published sources consulted. One is recorded as UNSUPPORTED (U2).
