# Try output and spacing

Read Foundations Basics unit 3, “Showing Your Work”. You can enter there without
reading earlier units. Keep its answer panels closed while making predictions.
You need paper or a text editor; no language installation is required.

1. Predict the complete output of `SHOW "6 + 2"` and `SHOW 6 + 2`. Explain the
   difference. Include any spaces or punctuation you expect to see.
2. We want `Lives: 3`. Predict `SHOW "Lives:", 1 + 2`, then repair it. Explain
   which characters in the instruction become visible output.
3. Predict `SHOW "Go ", "now"` and `SHOW "Go", "now"`. Does a space outside
   the quotes, after the comma, change either result? Explain your answer.
4. Optional transfer: read “Give the words somewhere to go” in
   [Ask and use the answer](https://code198x.com/systems/sinclair-zx-spectrum/basic/meet-basic/unit-03-ask-and-use-the-answer/).
   Compare `PRINT "Lives: ";1+2` with `PRINT "Lives: ",1+2`. Which one joins
   immediately? What would you check before copying this idea to another language?

Record your first answers before opening explanations. If you change an answer,
keep both versions and say what changed your mind. Reading the optional BASIC
explanation does not require running its whole story.

A parent or teacher can give these prompts and note where help was needed.
Please let the learner explain before offering a correction. The separate
[assessor notes](ASSESSOR.md) contain answers and observation guidance.
