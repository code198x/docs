## What this is

Names and subjects the Vault **already cites** — usually in bold, in an entry that has been
grounded in period sources — but which have **no entry of their own**. Every item here was
discovered while writing something else, which is why it needs recording: the demand is real
and documented, but it surfaces one entry at a time and gets lost.

The list is generated by scanning bolded proper nouns across vault entries and subtracting
everything that already has a slug in any category, then curating by hand. The raw scan
mixes people with game titles and product names, so it cannot be used unfiltered.

## Method note

Entries discovered this way are worth prioritising over working a category alphabetically.
Every item here is already load-bearing somewhere — an entry has made a claim that depends on
it. Writing them closes a citation that currently goes nowhere, and repeatedly forces the
existing entries to agree with each other, which is where several corrections have come from.
